Table rules

  • 6 decks; reshuffle at 75% shoe penetration
  • Dealer stands on soft 17
  • Blackjack pays 3:2
  • Double on any first two cards
  • Double after split allowed
  • Up to 4 hands per round
  • Splits require the same rank
  • Split aces receive one card; no re-splitting aces
  • Dealer checks for blackjack before player decisions
  • No surrender or insurance

Results

Hand stats count each split hand separately. Round stats combine all hands from the original deal: a winning split hand plus a losing split hand can be a zero-net round, but neither hand is counted as a push.

Return as the simulation grows

The line shows cumulative player profit as a percentage of starting bets. It is more informative than win rate because it includes pushes, 3:2 blackjacks, splits, and doubles.

How to read the results

  • House edge: is the estimated house advantage per original one-unit bet. A negative result means the player finished ahead in this sample.
  • Total action: units includes the extra stakes added by splits and doubles.
  • Zero-net rounds: these are not necessarily pushes. They can include a mix of wins and losses across split hands.
